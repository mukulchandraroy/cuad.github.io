About CUAD

Chittagong University Students' Association of Dimla (CUAD) is a student association that brings together students of Dimla who are studying at the University of Chittagong.

CUAD aims to create a strong and supportive community among students, promote collaboration, encourage academic and extracurricular development, and organize activities that strengthen the connection among students and alumni.

---

Our Mission

To build a strong, supportive, and connected community of Dimla students at the University of Chittagong while creating opportunities for academic, personal, cultural, and social development.

---

Our Vision

To establish CUAD as a strong and inclusive platform where students can connect, collaborate, develop their potential, and contribute positively to their community.

---

Our Activities

CUAD organizes and supports various activities, including:

- Academic and educational programs
- Freshers' reception and farewell programs
- Cultural activities
- Social and community initiatives
- Student welfare activities
- Networking and social gatherings
- Career and development activities
- Events and celebrations
